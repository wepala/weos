## Ask for a commercial license

Contact Wepala at `<contact>`. Tell us what you are building, and how you run
or ship weos (a hosted service, on-premises, or inside another product).

## Contributions

Wepala can offer both licenses only if it has the right to license every
contribution both ways. Contributors therefore agree to the
[Individual Contributor License Agreement](CLA.md) before their first pull
request is merged. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Third-party modules

The commercial license covers weos itself. Its dependencies stay under their
own licenses — for example, [pericarp](https://github.com/akeemphilbert/pericarp)
is MIT-licensed. See [`go.mod`](go.mod) and
[`web/admin/package.json`](web/admin/package.json).
